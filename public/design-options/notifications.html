Notification styles

Five ways to upgrade the console's notifications. Each preview below works: click Next or Validate inside it, or use the buttons under it. Hover a notification to pause its timer.

Today !Data Set Please validate the credentials first.

No close button, no action, no sign of how long it stays, and it repeats on every click.

A
Refined pill
Same dark pill at the top, upgraded
Smallest change

What's new

  • A ring around the icon counts down. Hover to pause it.
  • One action button inside (Validate now, Undo) and a close ×.
  • Repeats merge into one pill with a count (×2) and a small shake instead of stacking copies.
  • Errors shake once when they appear. Loading turns into the result in place.
Best for: a quick upgrade that keeps the current look and position.
B
Card stack
White cards in the bottom-right, like Linear or Vercel
Recommended

What's new

  • A title, a one-line explanation and up to two buttons.
  • Several notifications fold into a neat stack. Hover to fan them out.
  • Drag a card to the right to swipe it away, or press ×.
  • A thin bar shows time left and pauses on hover. Loading turns into the result in place.
Best for: most product messages: saves, deletes, background jobs, connection tests.
C
Morphing island
One dark capsule that grows and shrinks, like Apple's Dynamic Island
Most distinctive

What's new

  • "Validating…" sits as a small pill, then springs open into the result.
  • Only one message shows at a time. Others queue behind it (+2) instead of piling up.
  • Actions and a timer line live inside the capsule.
Best for: a signature look, and async actions like validate, test connection and scan.
D
Contextual
Show the problem where it is, like Google's Material forms
Recommended for forms

What's new

  • No floating toast for form mistakes. The button that needs attention shakes, glows and gets a callout with the fix.
  • The validation result appears right next to the Validate button.
  • Other messages appear as a banner at the top of the card. It pushes content down instead of covering it.
Best for: wizard and form errors, like the one in your screenshot.
E
Cards + inbox
Cards that file themselves under a bell, like Slack or GitHub
Keeps history

What's new

  • The same cards as B, but when they leave they fly into a bell in the top bar.
  • The bell counts unread items and keeps a history, so a missed toast isn't lost.
  • Open the bell to filter, mark as read or remove.
Best for: events people come back to: scans, alerts, exports, license notices.

My pick: B + D

D for form and wizard mistakes, like "validate the credentials first". The fix sits next to the button, so people don't need to read a toast and hunt for the cause.

B for everything else: saves, undo, background jobs and connection results. The bell from E can be added later if people need a history.
